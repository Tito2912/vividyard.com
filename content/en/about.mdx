## About Vividyard

**Vividyard** is an independent site dedicated to AI creative tools: stock images, AI image generators, design tools, and visual resources for creators, entrepreneurs, and marketing teams.

### Our mission

Help content creators, marketing teams, and entrepreneurs choose the right visual tools — without getting lost in endless catalogues.

### Business model

Vividyard uses **affiliate links**. If you purchase or sign up through our links, we earn a commission from our partners (Vecteezy, rawpixel, Shutterstock, Placeit, Adobe…) at no extra cost to you.

Our reviews are written independently — we do not accept payment in exchange for positive coverage.
