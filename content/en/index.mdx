## Why use AI creative tools?

Visuals drive every campaign, website, and social post. AI creative tools let you:

- **Find quality stock images** without agency-level pricing
- **Generate custom visuals** with AI tools like Midjourney or Adobe Firefly
- **Create mockups and templates** quickly with tools like Placeit

## Our selection

### Stock images & creative resources

**[Vecteezy](/en/blog/vecteezy-review)** — Massive library of vectors, photos, and video. Generous free plan with commercial license.

**[rawpixel](/en/blog/rawpixel-review)** — Rights-cleared images with a strong creative and editorial focus. Competitive pricing.

**[Shutterstock](/en/blog/shutterstock-review)** — Global stock imagery leader. Premium quality, millions of files, flexible plans.

### Design & templates

**[Placeit by Envato](/en/blog/placeit-review)** — Mockups, logos, banners, and animated videos. Perfect for marketing content without a designer.

### AI image generation

**[Adobe Firefly](/en/blog/adobe-firefly-review)** — AI image generation built into Adobe. Commercially safe output.
