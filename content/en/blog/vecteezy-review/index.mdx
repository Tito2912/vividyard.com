## What is Vecteezy?

Vecteezy launched in 2008 primarily as a vector graphics library. Over the years it expanded into photos, video clips, and digital backgrounds, making it a broad-purpose creative resource. Today it hosts over 60 million files across its free and premium tiers.

The platform is popular with graphic designers, social media managers, marketers, and small businesses that need a steady flow of quality visuals without a large budget.

## Who Should Use Vecteezy?

**Vecteezy works well for:**
- Graphic designers and illustrators who live in the vector world
- Marketers creating social content who can add an attribution line
- Developers building apps or websites who need scalable SVG assets
