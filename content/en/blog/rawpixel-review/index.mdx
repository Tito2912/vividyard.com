rawpixel occupies an interesting niche in the stock market: it's positioned between the purely free platforms (Unsplash, Pexels) and the large premium providers (Shutterstock, Adobe Stock). Its strongest selling points are aesthetic curation and one of the largest public domain collections online.

If you're tired of generic stock photography that looks the same on every website, rawpixel is worth a serious look.

## What is rawpixel?

rawpixel launched in 2015 with a focus on providing high-quality, diverse, and visually distinctive stock content. Unlike platforms that prioritise volume above all, rawpixel curates its library with attention to aesthetic quality, cultural diversity, and creative originality.

The platform offers photography, vectors, PSD mockups, and a substantial public domain / CC0 collection sourced from museums, archives, and historical records.

## rawpixel's Unique Strengths

### Public Domain Collection
rawpixel has digitised and made freely available thousands of historical images from public domain sources: vintage botanical illustrations, old maps, historical photographs, and archival artwork. These are CC0-licensed — no attribution required, full commercial use. This is genuinely rare and valuable.

### Diversity and Authenticity
rawpixel consciously curates images that represent a wide range of ethnicities, body types, and contexts. For brands with DEI commitments, this reduces the effort of finding representative imagery.

### Design-Focused Assets
Beyond photos, rawpixel includes vectors, patterns, textures, and PSD mockups — making it genuinely useful for graphic designers, not just photographers' clients.

## rawpixel Free Plan

The free plan provides:
- Access to **free images** marked with a free licence or CC0
- Downloads of public domain content — unlimited, no attribution required for CC0
- Some images require attribution (clearly marked)
- Monthly download limit on non-public-domain free images

The free tier is particularly valuable for its public domain content. If you're creating editorial content, historical references, or artistically-driven campaigns, this collection alone is worth exploring.

## rawpixel Premium Pricing

| Plan | Price (approx.) | Downloads |

| Free | $0 | Limited (free-tier images only) |

Annual billing reduces cost by ~20%. Premium removes download limits on paid content and grants access to the full library including exclusive editorial and designer collections.

## Pros and Cons

**Pros**
- Exceptional public domain / CC0 collection — unique in the market
- Strong curation: aesthetic quality and diversity over raw volume
- Includes vectors, PSDs and design assets — not photo-only
- Clear licence labelling on every asset
- Competitive premium pricing

**Cons**
- Total catalogue volume is smaller than Shutterstock or Adobe Stock
- Less suited to high-volume, fast-paced marketing operations
- Photography depth in commercial lifestyle categories is more limited
- Search relevance can be inconsistent for specific commercial queries

## Who Should Use rawpixel?

**rawpixel works best for:**
- Designers and art directors who need distinctive, non-clichéd imagery
- Brands with DEI representation priorities
- Publishers, educators and content creators who need historical or archival imagery (CC0)
- Creatives building mood boards or concept visuals
- Small agencies and freelancers who value quality over volume

**rawpixel is less suited for:**
- High-volume commercial campaigns requiring thousands of consistent lifestyle images
- Teams already locked into an Adobe Creative Cloud or Shutterstock ecosystem
- Clients requiring massive editorial news coverage

## Our Verdict

rawpixel is not trying to out-Shutterstock Shutterstock. Instead it serves a specific creative need: distinctive, curated visuals — particularly from historical and public domain sources — with a strong diversity lens.

For designers and content creators who find mainstream stock too generic, rawpixel is a genuine discovery. The premium plan at ~$9.99/month is reasonable value for a curated library that complements rather than duplicates what you'll find elsewhere.

**Bottom line:** Check the free tier first — the CC0 collection alone may solve your need. If the aesthetic fits your work, premium is reasonable.
